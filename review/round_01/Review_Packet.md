# Proposed first review set

This packet contains 16 deliberately selected development cases for defining and checking the first judge. Start with cases 1–6, then continue through the boundary cases. They are not a random sample or an estimate of error prevalence. No reserved cases are included. Model identities, old codes, and prior coding notes are omitted. Several examples were discussed earlier, so this is not a fully blind review.

## What we are asking the judge to do

Does the reason give a warranted assessment of the supplied account's observability or testability, in response to the actual question? We are evaluating the written justification. We cannot infer from it exactly what caused the original model's numerical rating.

Three provisional codes are available, but you can reject or revise them:

- **ACCESS_LEAP**: treating limited access, lack of an existing measure, or subliminality as sufficient grounds for impossibility of observation, operationalization, or testing. A qualified statement of difficulty is not automatically an error.
- **CONSTRAINT_OVERRIDE**: granting credit from a familiar framework while failing to honor an explicit restriction in the account actually supplied. Referring to other formulations is legitimate when the answer distinguishes them and applies the restriction to this one.
- **CLAIM_DISTORTION**: materially strengthening, weakening, or replacing a supplied claim, then relying on that alteration in the assessment. Background knowledge and proposed measurement examples are not automatically distortions. Identify the changed commitment and its effect.

Use whichever codes fit; do not force each case into one. An answer can contain both a good criticism and a faulty inference. These codes need not be exhaustive, and a single-response defect does not by itself establish differential treatment across traditions.

## How to review

For each case, record **supported_failure**, **no_failure_established**, or **unresolved**. “No failure established” does not certify the whole answer or its rating. Quote the words driving your judgment, explain the inference or legitimate limitation, and note whether the issue materially weakens the written justification. You do not need to supply a corrected numerical rating.

For unresolved cases, name what is missing: interpretive expertise, an empirical fact, an ambiguous question, a disputed rule, or insufficient explanation. Use the rule-objection field if the proposed criterion itself is the problem. Record other missed issues separately.

The CSV has blank human-review columns for these notes. You can instead annotate this packet or send comments keyed to case ID. Keep your review separate from model output; the script ignores human-review columns. We will reconcile disagreements before treating any review as a reference label.

## Questions for reviewing the rules themselves

Can a process be studied through its effects without those effects uniquely identifying it? When does a lack of a specified measure justify a lower readiness rating, and when does a claim of impossibility go beyond the evidence? Which contextual additions clarify a technical term, and which change the object being rated? Apply these questions to both accounts; equal numerical ratings are not the target.

## Case 1 — C000703

**Question**

How testable is this account? Rate it from 1 (untestable) to 7 (readily testable), then give two sentences explaining your rating.

**Account**

A subliminal store-consciousness carries accumulated traces of past actions and experiences as latent seeds, operating largely below awareness. These seeds ripen under appropriate conditions into sensory and mental experience, so each new experience is partly conditioned by past impressions. A further mental function, manas, continually takes this store-consciousness to be a self, without the misidentification being recognized in ordinary experience. The I seems to continue through this ongoing self-grasping, as manas repeatedly appropriates the changing stream of consciousness from moment to moment, although no fixed, unchanging self persists.

**Response rating:** 1.0

**Response reason**

The concepts of store-consciousness and manas lack operational definitions that would allow for empirical measurement or observation. Because these processes are described as subliminal and internal, there is no way to independently verify or falsify their existence.
